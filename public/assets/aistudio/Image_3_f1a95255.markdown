## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conviction, authoritative visual language for enterprise SaaS platforms and executive digital solutions. It bridges structural restraint with high-energy digital accents, evoking confidence, clarity, and momentum.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate SaaS** with luminous tonal gradients and tactile micro-details:
- **Clean Architectural Canvases**: Warm mineral white backgrounds and structured modular cards preserve readability and cognitive ease.
- **Deep Velocity Gradients**: Rich cobalt and midnight blues create deep focal anchors for key interactive stages, stat banners, and hero modules.
- **Precision Energy**: High-visibility habañero orange acts as an assertive catalyst for conversion triggers, status indicators, and critical metrics.
- **Executive Finishes**: Pill-shaped control mechanisms, frosted glass overlays, and diffused indigo ambient shadows provide a refined, tactile consumer-grade enterprise experience.

## Colors

The palette synthesizes deliberate contrast: foundational deep royal pigments against warm architectural minerals, punctuated by high-conversion habañero ember.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#223382` - Deep Space Royal)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, structural brand identity, active navigation states, and headline accentuation. When paired with `Deadly Depths (#111144)`, it forms the brand's signature linear and radial background gradients.
- **Secondary (`#F98513` - Habañero Orange)**: High-priority conversion moments, live notification badges, critical progress metrics, and key marketing badges. Used sparingly to preserve maximum focal intensity.
- **Tertiary (`#9BACD8` - Aster Flower Blue)**: Secondary badges, subtle interactive states, icon container fills, hover highlights, and tinted gradient stops.
- **Neutral (`#DAD1C8` - Jodhpur Tan)**: Soft borders, divider lines, disabled UI structures, and subdued background elevations.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F4F1EC` - Luster White)**: The fundamental warm foundation for page backgrounds, card surfaces, and input canvas containers, offering superior eye comfort over sterile neutral whites.
- **Deep Contrast (`#111144` - Deadly Depths Navy)**: High-contrast typography, footer shells, and deep immersion metric containers.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to maintain a modern, friendly yet authoritative SaaS appearance. 

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly kerned with negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver confident, editorial punch across value propositions and section headers.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for maximum legibility on both warm light canvases and immersive blue gradient surfaces, using balanced line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) and subtle gray-navy text tones (`#111144` at 80% opacity).
- **Stat Callouts**: Bold, high-visibility weights paired directly with reduced label captions (`label-caps`) to produce high-impact quantitative proof points.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system centered within a max-width container of `1240px`.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: Full 12-column configuration with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` side canvas margins. Multi-column cards (3-card or 4-card metric strips) sit horizontally side-by-side.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins. Multi-column stat groups adapt to a balanced 2x2 grid.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Data cards, stat groups, and split-screen features collapse into a unified single-column vertical flow.

### Rhythm
Components rely on strict 8pt base unit multipliers. Primary interactive controls adhere to 48px standard touch targets on mobile and 44px on desktop. Section blocks are separated by `space-2xl` to give room to deep gradient cards and stat callouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through luminous tonal surfaces, subtle glass borders, and tinted ambient shadows rather than traditional murky gray shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Baseline `#F4F1EC` Luster White canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Panes)**: `#FFFFFF` pure white fill with a delicate `1px` stroke using `#DAD1C8` at 60% opacity, elevated by a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(34, 51, 130, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Popovers & Hover Cards)**: Pure white or frosted backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(34, 51, 130, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Immersive Focal Containers)**: Linear gradient surfaces transitioning from `#223382` (Deep Space Royal) to `#111144` (Deadly Depths), bordered by a semi-transparent luminous inner stroke (`rgba(155, 172, 216, 0.25)`), creating an ambient blue glow: `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(17, 17, 68, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry for all primary interactive elements and navigation anchors, paired with soft, rounded container geometry for structural panels.

- **Buttons & Chips**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) provide sleek modern ergonomics and clear affordance.
- **Cards & Banners**: Elevated cards utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to retain an inviting, contemporary tech presence.
- **Feature Modules & Stat Banners**: Outer gradient containers utilize 28px–32px radii, mirroring top-tier SaaS design patterns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), background in Deep Space Royal (`#223382`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#111144` with a subtle lift shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(34, 51, 130, 0.3)`.
- **Accent Conversion**: Background in Habañero Orange (`#F98513`), text in `#FFFFFF`, used exclusively for high-intent actions ("Commencer maintenant", "Start Free Trial").
- **Secondary Outline**: Fully rounded, transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#223382` or `#DAD1C8`, text in `#223382`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Fully rounded, no border, text in `#223382` with an accompanying directional arrow icon (`→`) that translates 4px on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- **Pill Badges**: Compact pill styling (`px-3`, `py-1`, `font-size: 12px`, bold).
  - *New/Active*: Solid `#223382` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
  - *Accent*: Light `#F98513` tint background (12% opacity) with solid `#F98513` text.
  - *Informational*: `#9BACD8` tint background (20% opacity) with `#223382` text.

### Stat Banner Cards
- Deep gradient container (`#223382` to `#111144`) with rounded 24px corners.
- Segmented stat columns divided by translucent vertical rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- Distinctive `#1` highlight card nested inside the container with glassmorphic backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) and white typographic hierarchy.

### Feature Cards & Lists
- **Feature Cards**: White surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px` subtle border in `#DAD1C8`, soft blue-tinted drop shadow, internal padding of `2rem`. Includes a circular or soft-square Aster Flower Blue (`#9BACD8`) tinted icon container.
- **Checklist Items**: Compact row layout featuring a solid royal blue circle badge with a white checkmark icon (`✓`), followed by `body-md` typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Rounded pill or 12px corners, warm white `#FFFFFF` surface with `#DAD1C8` border. Focus state triggers a 2px outer ring in `#223382` with 20% opacity blur.
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes**: 20px controls with a 2px `#DAD1C8` stroke. Checked state transitions to Deep Space Royal (`#223382`) with clean white indicators.
